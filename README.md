# The Proof of the Hodge Conjecture

**Author:** Zhang Xun · **ORCID:** [0009-0004-5396-8952](https://orcid.org/0009-0004-5396-8952)

## Abstract

This paper presents the projection and proof of the Hodge Conjecture within the FBGT (Fiber Bundle Gauge Theory) framework. The core tools are the method of moving frames, the BIBD–Stokes isomorphism, and the functor from algebraic cycles to prime ideal chains. The central projection result is


that is: the spectral components corresponding to Hodge classes equal the rational linear combinations of the spectral components of algebraic cycles. The paper is organized into thirteen parts, including the standard statement, FBGT positioning, moving frames, BIBD–Stokes isomorphism, the algebraic cycle functor, the complete projection, Hodge spectral discreteness, the reverse inclusion, three constructions requiring rigorous treatment, and the final proof. Rigor relies on the consistency of axioms A.0–A.20 (guaranteed by ZFC+supALG) and on standard results in complex geometry, Hodge theory, and algebraic geometry.

**Keywords:** Hodge Conjecture; FBGT; moving frames; BIBD–Stokes isomorphism; algebraic cycles; prime ideal chains; spectral decomposition
